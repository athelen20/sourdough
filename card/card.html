Printing these cards

Four cards per sheet, 4.25 × 5.5 inches each (a quarter of a Letter page). Page 1 is the front, page 2 is the back.

  • Print double-sided, flip on the long edge.
  • Scale: 100% — not "fit to page", or the QR code shrinks.
  • Turn on "Background graphics" in the print dialog.
  • Margins: None.
  • All four cards on a sheet are identical, so the back lines up whichever way your printer flips.
  • Cut along the dashed lines. Card stock works nicely but plain paper is fine.

Test one sheet first and scan the QR code with your phone before printing the rest.

Waking Snow up

You need a scale, a jar, bread flour and a warm spot.

  1. Day 1. Tip the flakes into a jar with 25 g warm water. Wait 2–4 h until dissolved, then stir in 25 g bread flour. Cover loosely, keep warm.
  2. Day 2. Probably nothing yet. Add 25 g water + 25 g flour.
  3. Day 3. Bubbles, odd smell. Now start discarding: keep 25 g, bin the rest, feed 25 g + 25 g.
  4. Days 4–7. Same again, every 12 hours.
Ready when it reliably doubles within 4–8 h, domes on top, and smells tangy rather than of solvent. Usually 5–7 days, sometimes 10.

Then keep it alive

Keep it in the fridge and feed once a week: keep 25 g, add 25 g water and 25 g flour. Before baking, feed it once or twice at room temperature to wake it up.

Don’t panic: dark liquid on top just means it’s hungry, and a sharp cheesy smell is normal early on. Only bin it for fuzzy mould or pink streaks.
