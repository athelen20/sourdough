Discard cheese crackers

Sharp, salty and very crisp - the homemade answer to the box of cheese crackers. One dough, two ways to shape it, and they disappear at roughly the same speed either way.

Uses discardCollected

  • 1 large tray
  • 30 min work
  • 325-350 °F / 163-175 °C

Ingredients

IngredientAmount
Sourdough discard, unfed120 g
Extra-mature cheddar, finely grated150 g
Butter, melted57 g
Plain flour180 g
Garlic powder1 tsp
Onion powder1 tsp
Fine salt½ tsp
Sugar, optional1 tsp

Grate the cheese yourself from a block. Pre-grated cheese is coated in starch to stop it clumping, and it makes noticeably duller crackers.

Method one · squares, no rolling

Faster, and the one to start with.

  1. Mix everything into a thick, spreadable batter. Too stiff? Add water a tablespoon at a time. Too loose? A little more flour.
  2. Line a large rimmed baking tray with parchment and spread the batter out very thinly and evenly, around 1.5 mm. Thin and even is the whole recipe.
  3. Bake at 163 °C / 325 °F for 7 minutes, until the surface looks set but not yet crisp.
  4. Take it out and score into 2.5 cm squares with a pizza wheel. Poke a hole in the centre of each with a cocktail stick.
  5. Back in for 15 to 30 minutes, turning the tray halfway, until golden and dry all the way to the middle.
  6. Cool completely on the tray, then snap along the lines.

Method two · cut into shapes

More work, considerably more fun, and the version children will want.

  1. Mix the same ingredients into a soft dough that holds together without being sticky.
  2. Wrap and chill at least 1 hour, up to a day. Chilling is what makes it roll cleanly and hold a shape.
  3. Roll out to about 3 mm on a lightly floured surface and cut out your shapes. Add an eye with a cocktail stick if you are making fish.
  4. Bake at 175 °C / 350 °F for 12 to 16 minutes, turning the tray halfway, until the edges are golden.
  5. Cool completely on the tray - they crisp as they cool.

Getting the crunch right

  • Thin and even matters more than anything else.
  • Bake until the centres are golden and dry, not just the edges. Underbaked crackers go soft within a day.
  • Turn the tray halfway. Every oven has a hot corner.
  • Judge them cold. They are never crisp straight out of the oven.

Variations

  • Swap the cheddar for parmesan, gouda or pepper jack.
  • Add rosemary, thyme, paprika or chilli flakes.
  • Both methods work with cold discard straight from the fridge.

Keeping them

Airtight at room temperature for up to a week. If they soften, 5 to 10 minutes at 150 °C brings the snap back.
